# Strategy tags: notes for Benjamin and Oliver

The parent desk shows “How they're solving it” once a strand has tagged answers. Under 8 tagged answers the line stays “Not enough to tell yet.”

## Samples

With the app running:

- `/preview/strategy?scene=pattern`
- `/preview/strategy?scene=mixed`

## What to watch

- The line should sound like a description, not a grade.
- A counting-on pattern on addition should mention grouping by tens next.
- A mix of ways should not name a winner.
- Compare the tag with what you saw the child do. The founder check is 10 questions: write the inferred tag beside your own note.

The dev practice screen records keypad order, erases, and whether Show me was opened. It does not show a clock.
